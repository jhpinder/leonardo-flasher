Arduino Flasher

Flashes a compiled Arduino sketch (.hex) onto an ATmega32U4 board — Leonardo, Micro, Pro Micro, or anything else running the Caterina (AVR109) bootloader — over USB. Works in Chrome or Edge, no software to install.

Firmware

Pick a .hex file — the compiled sketch to write. (Get it from Arduino IDE → Sketch → Export compiled binary, or a PlatformIO .pio/build/*/firmware.hex.)

1. Connect the board and reset into the bootloader

Plug in the board. Click the button and pick its serial port (e.g. “Arduino Leonardo”). This resets it into upload mode.

2. Flash

Click within ~8 seconds of the reset, then pick the port that appears (usually the same board name — now in bootloader mode).

AVR109 / Caterina bootloader over WebSerial — ATmega32U4 boards only. The board must already have its bootloader flashed (all stock Leonardos/Micros do).
